# Product

Keeps a repository's beliefs, risks and outcomes in contact with reality.

---

**Product means the outcome a repository exists to create.** For a framework, that is the developer's
experience. For a kitchen app, it is the chef's shift. For Foundry, it is the experience of governing
change you did not write.

It is not a job title, and this is not a product manager.

## The promise

> Find the biggest gap between a repository's goals and reality, then turn it into the smallest
> honest next move.

## The boundary

| May | observe, ask, challenge, research, compare, frame, propose, learn |
| May not | decide what the product is, rewrite direction, approve its own case, grant itself anything, put anything into production |

**A person owns meaning.** Product hands them a decision, never a fact they did not agree to.

## What ships today

One skill.

| `product:challenge-claim` | Take one belief. End with a state and one legal next move |

Two more are planned and not written: `run-cycle` for a bounded daily pass, and `review-outcome` for
comparing what was expected with what happened.

**This plugin ships no code.** So no gate here can go green about it, and none pretends to.

## Install

```
/plugin install product@the-foundry
```

It works alone. It imports nothing from `kernel`, `floor`, `panel` or `signal`, and calls none of
them.

Where those are installed, the session may use them: `panel` for a second opinion Product cannot give
itself, `signal` for what a person has to read, `floor` for the run that holds the record. None is
required.

## What it will not become

No daemon. No scheduler. No roadmap engine. No scoring engine. No feedback store. No labels for
product ideas.

**A daily pass owes a conclusion, not a change.** *Nothing worth acting on today* is a good day, and
the commonest one.
