# Goals are canonical in `goals.md`

The owner settled where goals live on 27 August 2026, in direction given to a run.

> Goals belong in .foundry/goals.md as the single canonical source. Doctrine holds stable identity,
> mission and principles, and links to goals without duplicating them.
>
> This approves the separation—not every proposed goal or every sentence in #391 and #382. Preserve
> useful content. Give goals stable IDs, priority, status and attributable acceptance. Unaccepted
> goals remain proposed.

## What it settles

One canonical source for goals. Doctrine links and never copies.

A goal carries a stable id, a priority, a state, and an acceptance naming who and when.

## What it does not settle

Any goal. This approved the structure alone.
