## What it settles

[Foundry uses Foundry](../goals/foundry-uses-foundry.md) is accepted, and it runs for one week from
7 September 2026.

**A gap that stops Foundry using Foundry jumps the queue.** The owner said so. They gave the
reason too: being ready for other repos.

**A brief opens work. It does not bound it.** One brief becomes several issues. Those become
charters, and a thing the size of a runtime earns more than one RFC first.

**Two hosts, one work source.** The Docker image is named as part of the goal, not a later nicety.

## What it does not settle

Which model judges what. The owner said to alternate where merited, and that Opus is often
enough. That is a call each time, never a rule.

**Which gap counts as one.** The goal says a gap that stops Foundry using Foundry jumps the queue.
It does not say who decides that a thing is such a gap, and a run calling its own work a blocker is
the obvious way to abuse it.

**What happens on the eighth day.** The last day is 14 September 2026, and that is arithmetic
rather than a decision — the owner said *the next week* and named no date.

**Whether this lapses, renews or becomes standing is unsettled**, and only the owner settles it. A
goal that quietly outlives its week is the same fault as a box nobody ticks: it stops being checked
and nobody notices.
