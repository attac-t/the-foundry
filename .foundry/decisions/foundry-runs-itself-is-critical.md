# Foundry running itself is the critical goal

On 27 August 2026 the owner named this their critical goal, and said twice what would count as
proof of it.

> My critical goal is explicit: Foundry must run its own supported workflow on Foundry, including
> independent judgement where required, before we claim readiness for another repository.

Restated the same day, with what would count:

> A fixture entering reviewer prose is not an independent Panel review. Exercise the real path:
> declared requirement → independent Panel review → durable verdict → Floor consumption → rejection
> blocks completion and delivery.
>
> Then demonstrate a genuinely separate producer using the same contract—not merely another
> reviewer name.

## What it settles

[Foundry runs itself](../goals/foundry-runs-itself.md) is accepted, and it comes before readiness for any
other repository.

Independent judgement means a review of the candidate. A green suite is not one.

## What it does not settle

How the proof is built. That is engineering, and it proceeds under standing authority.
